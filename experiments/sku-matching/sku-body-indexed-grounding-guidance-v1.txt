Grounding and exact quotation guidance (all semantic judgments remain model-authored)

Assess every complete option independently using the whole supplied context. Choose the grounding route that actually supports your judgment. Source verification establishes origin, not semantic truth, scope, equivalence, or exclusion.

context.target_selected_facts contains the supplied facts for the actual selected target SKU. When your judgment follows from these facts, use selected_fact and explicitly author the relevant target_fact_indices and all scope/relation/exclusivity explanations. selected_fact cites only supplied facts: author body_quote_refs=[], body_condition_bindings=[], and body_invariant=null. It does not require a quotation from the product body. Do not invent a body binding to restate an already supplied fact.

selected_sku_body has a stronger citation contract. Every body_condition_bindings entry must name a supplied target_fact_index. body_axis_quote must exactly equal that fact's axis_name or axis_name_span.quote, and body_value_quote must exactly equal its entire value or value_span.quote. Both must occur in a submitted exact body quotation. Bind every chosen fact exactly once and explain the product-attribute role for a decisive judgment. A semantically plausible description alone does not satisfy this literal binding contract. If that body route cannot be grounded, assess other supplied evidence independently; if positive grounding is insufficient, author unknown.

For each body quotation, use only {body_container_index:0, block_index:N, quote:TEXT, start:S, end:E}. N indexes context.eligible_target_body.blocks and must be in bounds. TEXT must exactly equal that block's support_text[S:E], with Unicode character offsets. Read the complete text first. You may calculate offsets for a quotation you have chosen, but code must not choose evidence, states, scopes, semantic relations, applicability, or winners.

For body_invariant, your authored attribute_quote and value_quote must be present literally in submitted body quotations. Explain the positive evidence for the target product, subject scope, quantifier scope, and applicability to every native state in exact order. Absence of an option axis does not establish invariance. A recommendation, comparison, related product, or restriction is not automatically a selected product attribute.

Keep the original option order and assess the whole value. Explicit conflict requires a direct contradiction and your non-uncertain exclusivity judgment with its explanation. A relative winner or an unknown alternative cannot exclude other options. Preserve the immutable first draft even when its citations or structure fail validation; do not retry or repair it.
