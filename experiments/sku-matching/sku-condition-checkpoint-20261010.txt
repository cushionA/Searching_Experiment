2026-10-10 SKU condition task checkpoint

Task contract
Product pairing is upstream. One case is one selected Rakuten SKU, including its
SKU price, versus the complete AU SKU array of one fixed AU URL. No sibling URL
rerouting. All conditions must hold on one row, including AU-only conditions.
Identity, price, availability and CSV eligibility are separate.

Operational tasks
1. Verify Rakuten-only conditions against the fixed AU title/current description;
   exclude an incompatible Rakuten SKU record, rather than deleting its axis.
2. Match the remaining selected SKU to one AU row using shared conditions.
The diagnostic implementation binds a partial row with shared axes first, then
checks missing/reverse conditions. A partial candidate is not a proved match.
